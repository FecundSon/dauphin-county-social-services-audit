# Dauphin County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-10-25 — AUDIT COMPLETE, awaiting Keith's accept checkpoint.
Scope: job centres, career training, resume/interview help, vocational
rehabilitation, and supported employment serving Dauphin County
(Harrisburg area). This file is the candidate list from public
directories; findings are in audit-report-2026-10-25.md (same folder).
Nothing has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation.

## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- Own site beats directory. Directory beats a third-party aggregator.
- No claim that an organisation is closed/defunct is made from a failed
  page load; such items are UNVERIFIED.
- A funder/operator name is not a second walk-in centre; a programme
  listed under a funder's name rather than the provider's name is a
  duplicate risk, flagged, not double-counted.
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms are NOT counted as public
  employment help (see Excluded section).
- ZIP code does not settle county — exact-address location does.
  Harrisburg-area listings in Camp Hill, Carlisle, Enola, and Lancaster
  are checked for county before counting.
- No safe-house or confidential location is in this file.
- This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident of Dauphin County.

## Excluded from this employment audit (not public employment help)
- Commercial staffing/recruitment firms with Harrisburg offices
  (Manpower, Aerotek and similar) — employers and recruiters, not
  free public employment-help services. They are not counted, and
  must not be presented as PA 211-style help.
- Individual employers' hiring pages and job boards (Indeed,
  LinkedIn, Career.io postings) — not services.
- The Dauphin County employment guide itself (dauphincounty.gov
  PDF) is an old candidate source, not a current directory; its
  phone numbers were not treated as verified.
