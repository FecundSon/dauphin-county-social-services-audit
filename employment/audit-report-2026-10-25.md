# Dauphin County, PA — Employment / Job Help Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-10-25
County: Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area). Category: employment / job help.
Method: pilot list built from public directories (statewide PA 211
search pages, PA CareerLink / SCPa Works lists, the older Dauphin
County employment guide as a candidate source only); every item then
checked against the organisation's own website/page, via public web
search and page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory
beats a third-party aggregator.
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211
or to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any
outward report. This audit does not refer to Keith as a local
resident of Dauphin County.
Recipient note: the directory is statewide PA 211 (pa211.org /
search.pa211.org) — NOT PA 211 East and NOT the Lancaster database
manager. PA 211's South Central region covers Dauphin, Cumberland,
Franklin, Perry, Adams, and York counties, which is why regional
listings routinely mix counties.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **PA CareerLink Harrisburg is confirmed — and it is the only
   Dauphin County CareerLink centre in this pilot.**
   Own PA CareerLink page: 100 N Cameron St Ste 101, Harrisburg,
   PA 17101, 717-783-3270, Mon–Fri 8am–4:30pm. PA 211's Harrisburg
   CareerLink entry matches it exactly. Do not add a second
   Harrisburg CareerLink from a satellite or programme name.
2. **SCPa Works is the board, not a second walk-in job centre.**
   Its own site gives 4201 Crums Mill Rd Ste 100A, Harrisburg,
   PA 17112, 717-236-7936, info@scpaworks.org, and describes
   SCPa Works (Southcentral Workforce Investment Board) as the
   body charged with managing federal/state/local workforce
   funding and overseeing six PA CareerLink offices across eight
   counties. Job seekers are routed to CareerLink
   (jobseekers@scpaworks.org). Listing SCPa Works as a separate
   walk-in centre double-counts one system.
3. **OVR Harrisburg: three Harrisburg addresses are three different
   things — do not merge them.**
   - District office (the walk-in/referral office for Dauphin and
     neighbouring counties): Forum Place, 555 Walnut St, 8th floor,
     Harrisburg, PA 17101, 717-787-7834, TTY 717-787-4013,
     toll-free 1-800-442-6352, RALIOVRIDHARRISBURG@PA.GOV.
   - Central office: 1521 N 6th St, Harrisburg, PA 17102,
     717-787-5244 / TTY 717-787-4885, toll-free 1-888-745-2357.
   - 651 Boas St, 7th floor, Harrisburg, is the Department of
     Labor & Industry building address used for the Bureau of
     Vocational Rehabilitation / central operations, not a
     separate district walk-in office.
   Directories that list only one of these without saying which
   office it is will misdirect callers.
4. **The "Harrisburg area" county trap — Camp Hill and Carlisle are
   Cumberland County, not Dauphin County.**
   - AHEDD Greater Harrisburg Area, 3300 Trindle Rd, Camp Hill,
     PA 17011, 717-731-5485 (own contact page) — serves Dauphin,
     Cumberland, Juniata, Perry and Lebanon, but its office is
     in Camp Hill, Cumberland County. Regional note only.
   - PA CareerLink Cumberland County, 40 Noble Blvd Ste 12,
     Carlisle, PA 17013, 717-243-4431 — Cumberland County.
   - Employment Skills Center, Carlisle — Cumberland County.
   - UCP Central PA HQ, 55 Utley Dr, Camp Hill, 717-737-3477 —
     Cumberland County; its employment programme is real, but
     Camp Hill must not be presented as a Dauphin centre, and
     its place-listed Harrisburg site (613 Sylvan Pl) is not
     own-site-confirmed as an employment intake.
   - IU13 Workforce Preparation is Lancaster-based; its in-person
     classes in these searches are at Lancaster and Lebanon
     CareerLinks. Dauphin residents are in its eligibility area,
     but there is no Dauphin centre on this evidence.
5. **Dress for Success: use PA 211's "call for information", not
   place-listing hours.**
   PA 211: Dress for Success South Central PA, Mobile Suiting
   Program, 29 S 10th St, Harrisburg, PA 17101, 717-232-1333,
   women, free, "call for information" — the mobile unit travels
   to partner programmes and CareerLinks and to Lebanon and York
   once a week. Place listings that give Mon–Fri 8am–4:30pm as
   boutique hours are not the PA 211 claim and should not be
   published as walk-in hours.
6. **CAPSTONE and CEEED are not general job help.**
   YWCA Greater Harrisburg's own programmes page lists
   Employment Programs generally, but its CAPSTONE programme is
   specifically supported employment/education for ages 16–26
   experiencing first-episode psychosis, with Dauphin County
   MH/ID and PPI — not a general job centre. CEEED (1301 Derry St,
   2016 list) has no current own-site programme found in these
   searches and is UNVERIFIED, not closed.
7. **AARP SCSEP in Harrisburg is UNVERIFIED as a local office —
   do not publish either old address.**
   Old lists conflict (2101 N Front St, Building 3, Ste 100,
   717-234-5961 vs 30 N 3rd St Ste 750). AARP's own SCSEP page
   confirms the national programme (55+, unemployed, low income,
   paid job training) but gives no Harrisburg office. A
   PA CareerLink SCSEP posting found in these searches lists
   eligible counties that do not include Dauphin — that posting
   is for a different sub-grantee area and is NOT proof Dauphin
   is excluded, but it is a reason not to publish a Dauphin
   SCSEP office without confirmation from the programme itself.
8. **Training programmes that have ended or moved must not be
   presented as current walk-in help.**
   HACC's SCPa Works partnership (CNC machining for 10 people)
   is a sponsored cohort, not an open walk-in service; HACC
   workforce training is enrolment-based at One HACC Dr,
   Harrisburg, PA 17110. DCTS (6001 Locust Ln, Harrisburg) is a
   full-time career-technical high school for Central Dauphin,
   Derry Township, Halifax, Lower Dauphin, Middletown and
   Susquehanna Township districts — not adult general job help.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. AARP
  SCSEP Harrisburg, CEEED, Catholic Charities employment placement,
  Easterseals Harrisburg, and Merakey Harrisburg are UNVERIFIED from
  own sites in these searches — that is a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding.
- It does not claim a programme exists because a directory lists it.
  TCCA, HACC, DCTS, the library, and the Arc are all real
  organisations whose general-employment-help status was not
  own-site-confirmed as a walk-in service; listing them as job
  centres would misdirect job seekers.
- It does not publish a funder/operator as a second centre
  (SCPa Works), a class site as a headquarters, a regional HQ as a
  local office (UCP Camp Hill; AHEDD Camp Hill), or a narrow
  programme as general help (YWCA CAPSTONE).
- It does not settle county from ZIP code. Camp Hill (17011),
  Carlisle (17013), Enola, and Lancaster addresses were checked by
  exact address, not by "Harrisburg area" grouping.
- It does not give employment or benefits advice. It audits contact
  information only: names, addresses, phones, hours, and intake
  routes as organisations themselves state them.
- It gives no street address for any domestic-violence safe house.
  No DV service is in this category, and none was needed.
- One page fetch in this run hit a rate limit (a Dauphin County
  employment-guide PDF re-read); that request was not retried, and
  no finding in this report depends on it — the guide was used
  only as a candidate source, with its entries re-checked
  elsewhere or marked UNVERIFIED.
