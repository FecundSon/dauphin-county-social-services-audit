# Dauphin County, PA — Food Pantry Audit Pilot (first audit under County Social Services Audit)
Date started: 2026-10-25
Scope: food pantries only, Dauphin County (Harrisburg area), Pennsylvania.
Nothing has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank,
or any pantry. No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
Keith is NOT a local resident of Dauphin County; this audit makes no claim
that he is.

## Directory note — read first (limitation, stated plainly)
The intended directory is the statewide PA 211 database (pa211.org /
search.pa211.org) — NOT PA 211 East, NOT the Lancaster database manager.
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven: on 2026-10-25 a text fetch
returned a PA 211 result for item 15 (Evangelical Free Church of Hershey)
and PA 211 taxonomy pages, but it did NOT return a complete, extractable
Dauphin County result list. The pilot below is therefore anchored on that
PA 211 item plus the secondary lists named under "Anchor sources", and
every item is labelled with where its directory claim came from. Items
whose claim comes only from a secondary list are marked as such — they
are candidates for PA 211 to confirm against its own database, not claims
that PA 211 lists them in that form. A live-browser check of
search.pa211.org for Dauphin County is still the one verification step
outstanding before any send.
