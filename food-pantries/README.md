# Dauphin County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Central Pennsylvania Food Bank,
or any pantry. No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 29 food pantry listings for Dauphin County, PA
(Harrisburg area), checked against the pantries' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it
does not claim to be a current list of open pantries, and it should not
be used as one without checking with the pantry or PA 211 first.
The auditor (Keith) is NOT a local resident of Dauphin County.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No pantry,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A pantry's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where a pantry's own site did not state an address or
  hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a
  gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be published
  as fact.
- **No pantry is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page, and a single third-party "temporarily closed" label
  (APPNA, Steelton), are reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a church/office/worship page are not pantry hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-10-25 did not return a complete
  extractable Dauphin County result list (one PA 211 result was
  retrieved: Evangelical Free Church of Hershey, 330 Hilltop Rd.).
  The pilot is therefore anchored on that PA 211 item plus secondary
  lists, each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser
  check of search.pa211.org for Dauphin County is still outstanding
  before any send to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the pantries themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
