# Dauphin County Food Pantry Audit — DRAFT, NOT SENT
**Date checked: 2026-10-25. Not sent to statewide PA 211, the Central
Pennsylvania Food Bank, or any pantry. Nothing was sent to anyone.**
**Method:** public web search + page-text fetch only. No pantry, PA 211,
or anyone else was contacted. A pantry's own website is treated as
authoritative over third-party directories. A failed or missing page is
reported as UNVERIFIED, never as closure. No pantry in this report is
called defunct or closed. Hours from a church/office page are not pantry
hours, and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
All hours and schedules below are date-stamped to 2026-10-25, never
presented as permanent.
**Auditor note:** Keith is NOT a local resident of Dauphin County.
This audit was prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

## Headline findings (the ones worth sending on, once Keith accepts
## and the PA 211 live-check caveat in the pilot list is resolved)
1. **Harrisburg Area Food Pantry — closing time and Saturday session
   corrected.** Directories say Mon–Fri 9:30am–12:00pm. The pantry's
   own site says **Mon–Fri 9:30am–12:30pm and the 3rd Saturday of the
   month 9:30am–12:30pm**, once every 30 days, no vouchers or
   appointments necessary. Source: hfoodpantry.org (fetched 2026-10-25).
2. **Harrisburg BIC pantry — TEMPORARILY RELOCATED; do not send people
   to 2217 Derry St. for the pantry on the strength of old listings.**
   The church's own Community Resources page gives the pantry as 2nd
   Thursdays 11am–2:30pm and 4th Thursdays 2–5:30pm, and states in bold
   **"TEMPORARILY RELOCATED* due to building damage"** — but does not
   state the relocated site in the fetched text. The Wednesday 5:30pm
   meal on the same page is a meal, not the pantry. Source:
   harrisburgbic.org/community-events/ (fetched 2026-10-25).
3. **Manna Food Pantry, Penbrook — appointment rule and hours corrected.**
   Directories say "Thursday 6–8pm." The pantry's own page says it is
   open **three Thursdays a month, appointment slots 6–7:30pm, an
   appointment is required every time**, one Thursday a month per
   household, for ZIPs 17103, 17104, 17109, at 56 Banks St., Harrisburg
   (Penbrook), call/text 717-602-5835. No walk-ins are served.
   The church phone 717-236-5275 is not the pantry phone. Source:
   penbrookucc.org/food-pantry/ (fetched 2026-10-25, incl. Oct–Dec 2026
   pickup calendar: Nov 5, 12, 19; Dec 3, 10, 17).
4. **Hummelstown Food Pantry — Thursday hours are gone; Wednesday runs
   to 5pm.** Secondary lists say Wed 9am–3:15pm and Thu 5–7:15pm. The
   pantry's own site announces a **change of hours: open only on
   Wednesdays, 9am–5pm; no hours on Thursdays until further notice**.
   Address 100 W. Main St., Hummelstown, 717-566-2589, for 17036
   residents. Source: hummelstownfoodpantry.org (fetched via search
   results 2026-10-25). Note: the change notice is undated in the
   fetched text, so PA 211 should confirm it is still current.
5. **Middletown has two different pantry claims at one address — do not
   merge them.** Fountain of Life Ministries Food Bank (290 Newberry
   Rd., 717-944-4455, Tue 6:30pm, 17057) and Oasis Community
   Partnership Food Pantry (same 290 Newberry Rd., 717-564-5003,
   Tue 11:30am–2pm, Fri 1–3:30pm, Sat 9am–12pm, by appointment) cannot
   both be published as settled facts from secondary sources alone.
   Own-site pantry pages were not found for either. PA 211 should
   confirm whether these are one pantry under two names, two
   programmes at one site, or a listing error.
6. **"Ecumenical Food Pantry," 901 N. 6th St., is probably not a separate
   pantry.** It shares the Harrisburg Area Food Pantry's phone
   (717-233-6889), while an older list names Messiah Lutheran Church
   at the same address with a different phone (717-233-6731) and a
   3-day supply once every 30 days. Listing it alongside HAFP as an
   independent pantry risks sending people to the same operation twice
   under two names. Recommendation: PA 211 to confirm or merge, not to
   publish both unexamined.
7. **Northern Dauphin appointment rule clarified.** The Central
   Pennsylvania Food Bank's own page confirms the Elizabethville pantry
   at 295 State Dr., its upper-Dauphin ZIP list (17005, 17023, 17030,
   17048, 17080, 17097, 17098, 17978, 17980), once a month, client
   choice, and **services by appointment only** — so the Mon–Fri
   7:30am–4pm hours on that page must not be read as walk-in
   distribution hours. Extended sessions: every 2nd Tuesday 11am–7pm
   and every 3rd Wednesday until 6pm. Source:
   centralpafoodbank.org/who-we-are/our-facility/ndfp-2/ (fetched
   2026-10-25). Pantry phone is not stated on that page: 717-905-2700
   (county centre) vs 717-905-2512 (secondary) remains unresolved.

## What this report does NOT claim
- No pantry is reported as closed/defunct. Every UNVERIFIED item is a
  gap in public web evidence, not a finding of closure — including the
  APPNA Steelton pantry, where one third-party "temporarily closed"
  label was explicitly NOT adopted.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a church/office/worship page are not pantry hours, and are
  flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (items 4, 7, 13,
  18, 23, and the hub).
- Secondary corroboration (even from two or three directories agreeing)
  is not own-site confirmation; items 11, 20, and 21 are CLARIFIED on
  that basis and should still be confirmed with the pantry.
- Schedules dated 2020 (items 16, 17) and undated change notices (item
  14) are flagged, not republished as current fact.
- ZIP code does not settle county: item 16 (Grace Chapel) is included
  on a township basis despite an Elizabethtown mailing address, and
  the out-of-scope list shows the reverse traps (Elizabethtown borough,
  Palmyra, and the West Shore are different counties).
- This report is not a directory and should not be used as a current
  list of open pantries without checking with the pantry or PA 211.
