Dauphin County pages old and new, secondary directories); every item
then checked against the organisation's own website/page, via public
web search and page-text fetch. Own site beats directory; directory

to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward

## Method note — page-fetch rate limit
Two page-text fetches late in this check were refused by the fetch
service with a rate-limit (429) error, not by the source sites. Those
two pages (the county's June 2024 cooling-centres release in full,
and the Leader Center's own homepage) were therefore evidenced from
search-result text of the same pages instead of a fresh full fetch.
Nothing in this report claims a live visit to a page that was only
search-snippet evidenced, and the two items most affected (Leader
Center hours/eligibility, Lykens days) are marked CLARIFIED, not
CONFIRMED, for that reason. No other fetch in this audit was refused.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
1. **The JCC Senior Adult Club moved — the old address is closed.**
   The Harrisburg JCC building at 3301 N Front St ceased operations
   effective September 28, 2024, and Senior Adult Club programming
   moved to the Grass Campus, 2986 N 2nd St, Harrisburg, from the
   week of September 29, 2024 (Federation's own transition notice).
   The club still meets Tuesdays and Thursdays with a kosher lunch
   (2025 programme guide: suggested donation $5 member / $11
   non-member; phone 717-236-9555). Any listing still sending
   seniors to 3301 N Front St for the senior programme is wrong.
2. **Mohler Senior Center is now the Leader Center for Active Life,
   at a new address.** The county's old page lists Mohler at
   25 Hope Dr, Hershey. The centre renamed for the Leader
   Foundation, moved into the Derry Township Community Center at
   605 Cocoa Ave, Hershey, and kept the same phone, 717-533-2002.
   Its own site describes a Hershey centre for people over 50
   (not the county centres' 60+ rule), Mon–Fri 8:00am–3:30pm.
   Publish "Leader Center for Active Life, 605 Cocoa Ave" and
   retire "Mohler, 25 Hope Dr" — do not list both as two centres.
3. **Northern Dauphin Human Services Center address corrected.**
   The June 2024 county cooling release gives 209 State St,
   Elizabethville. The county's current centre page and later
   county articles give 295 State Dr, Elizabethville, phone
   717-905-2700, lobby Mon–Fri 8:00am–4:30pm, 14 agencies under
   one roof (mental health, drug & alcohol counseling, food
   pantry, services for families, seniors, veterans). Publish
   295 State Dr, not 209 State St.
4. **Friendship Senior Center hours and age rule corrected.**
   The county old page says Mon–Fri 8:00am–3:30pm; the 2024
   cooling release says 9:00am–3:30pm. The township's own centre
   page says Mon–Fri 7:30am–3:00pm, for people 55 and over, in
   the East Annex (senior centre) of the Friendship YMCA at
   5000 Commons Dr, Harrisburg, PA 17112, phone 717-657-1547,
   $15 yearly membership, all-volunteer organisation, membership
   NOT limited to Dauphin County residents. Do not publish the
   YMCA's own fitness-centre hours (5:30am–9:00pm) as centre hours.
5. **The county's own centre count does not agree with itself —
   do not publish "14 senior centres."** The old county page
   claims fourteen sites but its fetched text lists eleven
   centres. The county's June 2024 release says "eight active
   Senior Centers": East Hanover, B'nai B'rith, JCC, Heinz-Menaker,
   Leader, Friendship, Lykens, Millersburg. The three old-page
   centres absent from the 2024 eight — Lick Towers, Hoy/Latsha
   (Steelton) and Bistline (Swatara) — are marked UNVERIFIED as
   current centre programmes below. They are NOT called closed:
   Hoy/Latsha hosted a governor's visit in June 2024, and all
   three sit inside housing properties where a meal site and a
   full centre are easily confused. PA 211 should confirm with
   the AAA which of the three still run a centre programme.
6. **Heinz-Menaker hours conflict three ways — publish no single
   hours figure.** Address 1824 N 4th St, Harrisburg, PA 17102
   and phone 717-238-7860 agree everywhere. Hours do not: county
   old page Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:00pm; aggregators Mon–Fri
   8:30am–3:00pm; June 2024 cooling release Tue–Fri
   8:30am–1:00pm. The centre is confirmed as a 2026 PA MEDI
   presentation venue, so it is active, but its current open
   hours need a fresh county confirmation.
7. **Lykens days conflict — same caution.** County old page:
   Mon–Fri 8:00am–1:00pm at 200 W Main St, Lykens, phone
   717-453-8350, meal served at Rattling Creek Apartments,
   15 S 2nd St, Lykens. June 2024 cooling release: Thursday and
   Friday only, same hours. Publish the address and phone, not
   the days, until the AAA confirms them.
8. **East Hanover: ignore the aggregator hours; keep the township
   distinction.** County old page: 8848 Jonestown Rd,
   Shellsville-Grantville, PA 17028, 717-469-7514, Mon/Wed/Fri
   9:00am–Noon. Aggregators print "10:00 AM – 4:00 AM" — an
   obvious AM/PM error, not centre hours. Note the township
   office phone is different (717-469-0833); use 717-469-7514
   for the centre. The centre's current name on township pages
   is "Autumn Leaves".
9. **Two different AAA-adjacent numbers must not be collapsed.**
   General AAA intake is 717-780-6130 (Mon–Fri 8:00am–4:30pm,
   emergency 24/7). PA MEDI local coordination is a different
   desk: Shannon Vallier, 717-780-6147, svallier@dauphincounty.gov
   (2026 Medicare 101 schedule; registration 24 hours ahead for
   presentations). A third number, 717-780-6131, appears only in
   the June 2024 release for fans/AC loans to seniors at or below
   150% of the Federal Poverty Line — do not publish it as a
   general AAA number.
10. **Dauphin's Farmers Market figure is $20, not $25.** The PA 211
    record for Dauphin County AAA Farmers Market Nutrition
    Program states $20 vouchers, June 1–September 30, first come
    first served, for county residents 60+ within annually
    determined income levels, call or walk in, proxy allowed with
    a signed form and photo ID (call ahead for the form).
    Another county's $25 figure must not be carried into Dauphin.
    Season dates still need a county-own-page confirmation for
    the current year before reuse.
11. **SeniorLAW is a statewide helpline for Dauphin, not a Dauphin
    office.** SeniorLAW Center's HelpLine is 1-877-727-7529
    (1-877-PA SR LAW), telephone intake Mon–Thu 10:00am–12:00pm,
    free/confidential legal advice, brief services, information
    and referrals for people 60+ in all 67 Pennsylvania counties,
    including the PA Grandparents Raising Grandchildren Legal
    Line. Extended individual representation is currently for
    Philadelphia-area counties only (per Penn State's programme
    page). Its office address (1650 Arch St, Philadelphia) must
    NOT be listed as a Dauphin location.
12. **Bistline, Lick Towers and Hoy/Latsha: housing phone ≠ centre
    phone.** Bistline House housing is 717-939-9301; the old
    county centre listing adds "ext 524" — unverified. Hoy Towers
    housing is also 717-939-9301 (same Housing Authority number),

    Directories that print the housing number as the centre

    UNVERIFIED as current centre programmes (see per-listing
    table), not closed.

  organisation's own page could not confirm that specific claim in
  this check — a gap in public web evidence, not a finding. In

  the county's eight-centre list may simply be the cooling-centre
  subset. Only the AAA can settle that.

  labelled directory-only must not be published as fact.

  operating, and seasonal wording (Farmers Market June–September)
  must be re-checked before reuse after its season/date.
- No health, benefits, or legal advice is given or rewritten here;
  contact information and listing accuracy only.
- Centre hours taken from the county's OLD domain page
  (dauphincounty.org) are treated as stale directory claims
  wherever a newer county source conflicts, not as current fact.
