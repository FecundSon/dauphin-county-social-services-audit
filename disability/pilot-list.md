## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- An organisation's own website/page is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. A failed or missing page is UNVERIFIED, never
  a closure finding. No organisation is called defunct/closed.
- Contact information only is audited. No health, benefits, or legal
  advice is rewritten.
- No domestic-violence safe-house address appears in this category.
- ZIP code does not settle county. County calls below rest on the
  organisation's own site or exact address where stated, and are
  flagged where an office sits outside Dauphin County but serves
  Dauphin County residents.
- Harrisburg-area trap: several "Harrisburg" services sit in
  neighbouring counties (Camp Hill = Cumberland County, Enola =
  Cumberland County, Penbrook = Dauphin County borough adjacent to
  Harrisburg city). They are kept in the list where they serve
  Dauphin County, but labelled as out-of-county offices.
