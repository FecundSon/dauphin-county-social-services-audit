## What this does NOT claim
- No organisation is called closed or defunct. UNVERIFIED means the
  own-site evidence was not found in this check, not that a service
  has ended. In particular, Merakey Harrisburg and ODHH Harrisburg
  are NOT claimed to be closed — their local details simply were
  not own-site confirmed here.
- Hours, suite numbers, extensions, and site addresses marked
  UNVERIFIED must not be published as fact from this audit; they
  need a live-browser or own-site re-check (notably: ODHH phone/
  address and relay routes, Merakey Harrisburg sites, CSG
  Harrisburg sites, Vision Resources hours/TTY, Rebuilding
  Together address/phone and application status, SSA Harrisburg
  via the ssa.gov locator, CAIU extension numbers, DRP suite
  number, Arc of Pennsylvania current street address).
- County calls rest on the organisations' own sites or exact
  addresses where stated; ZIP alone was never used to settle
  county. Camp Hill and Enola addresses are Cumberland County
  even though directories file them under "Harrisburg area."
- This audit checks contact information only. It does not assess
  service quality, eligibility outcomes, or benefits/legal advice.
- Accessible-contact note (section 10 watch item): TTY/relay
  numbers are the likeliest stale detail in this category. The
  only TTY numbers published as current here are those on
  TechOWL's own ATRC page (UCP Camp Hill TTY (717) 737-0158),
  the OVR handbook TTYs, and SSA's national TTY
  1-800-325-0778. Older TTY numbers (e.g. OVR 717-787-4885,
  TechOWL ATRC ext. 152) are flagged as superseded, not carried
