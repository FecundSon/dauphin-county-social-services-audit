# Dauphin County, PA — Disability Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: NOT SENT. Nothing in this audit has been
sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 27 disability services listings serving
Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is a
curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be
a current list of open services, and it should not be used as one
without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
  In particular, Merakey Harrisburg and the PA Office for the Deaf
  and Hard of Hearing are UNVERIFIED on local details here, not
  closed.
- Late in this check the page-fetch service rate-limited (429);
  affected requests were not retried. Details not confirmed from
  an own-site page are marked UNVERIFIED in the full report and
  must not be published as fact; a live-browser re-check is the
  remaining step for ODHH, Merakey and CSG Harrisburg sites,
  Vision Resources hours/TTY, Rebuilding Together, and the SSA
  locator.
- Many items carry UNVERIFIED details (hours, a suite, an
  extension) — listed per item in the report, never published
  as fact.
- ZIP code was never used to settle county: Camp Hill and Enola
  offices are in Cumberland County although they serve Dauphin
  County, and are labelled as such.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections,
  challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211 or the
  organisations themselves are welcome — that is the point of
  publishing it first before any send.
