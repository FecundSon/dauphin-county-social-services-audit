# Dauphin County, PA — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 26 substance use treatment / recovery listings
for Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public listings, anchored on the
Dauphin County Department of Drug and Alcohol Services provider list. It
is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to
be a current list of open services, and it should not be used as one
without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first. No bed/placement
availability is reported — intake process only. Keith is NOT a local
resident of Dauphin County.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories; the county provider list is treated as a
  directory claim to be checked, not as own-site proof.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
  Three page fetches late in the run were refused by the fetch service
  with a rate limit; that is a tool limit, not evidence about those
  organisations, and the affected items are flagged for a live-browser
  re-check before any PA 211 submission.
- No treatment or medical advice is rewritten here; contact
  information only.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
