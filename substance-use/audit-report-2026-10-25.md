# Dauphin County, PA — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-10-25
County: Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area). Category: substance use
treatment / recovery.
Method: pilot list built from the Dauphin County Department of Drug and
Alcohol Services provider list (dauphincounty.gov), PA 211 listings and
public directories; every item then checked against the organisation's
own website/page, via public web search and page-text fetches. Own site
beats directory; directory beats a third-party aggregator.
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or
to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
report. This audit is contact information only: it does not rewrite
treatment or medical advice, and it does not report bed/placement
availability — intake process only. Keith is NOT a local resident of
Dauphin County.
Caveat on method: three page-text fetches late in the run (Daystar,
RASE contact page, county treatment-services page) were refused by the
fetch service with a rate limit (429). That is a tool limit, not evidence
about those organisations. Where an own-site page could not be fetched,
the verdict rests on the county list plus directory agreement and is
marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED, never CONFIRMED on a fetch that did not
happen. Those items are listed for a live-browser re-check before any
PA 211 submission.

someone trying to use the service. All other items are in the

3. **Gaudenzia Harrisburg Outpatient — use (717) 238-4200 and the

   Its own contact page lists "The RASE Project – Main Office, 5525

   The same phone at the Cameron St address is also the source of the

8. **Firetree Conewago Place — the county's (888) 347-3873 is the

9. **"Substance Abuse Services Inc" and "Discovery House Harrisburg"

10. **Elizabethville has one site, not one listing: 295 State Dr is

11. **Evergreen House phone is in conflict — do not publish a single
    number yet.** The county list gives (717) 657-4804 for 120 Willow Rd
    Suite B, Harrisburg, PA 17109, halfway house, female only; most
    directories give (717) 412-4594 at the same address, and an old
    100 Evergreen St / (717) 238-6343 listing still circulates. Address

12. **West-shore listings are not Dauphin listings.** New Insights II /
    Invision (716 State St, Lemoyne), ARS (Camp Hill) and Mechanicsburg
    CTC are all Cumberland County; Naaman Elizabethtown is Lancaster
    County. Any "Harrisburg area" grouping that files them under Dauphin
    County misdirects a Dauphin resident to another county's services.

## What this report does NOT claim
- No organisation is called closed or defunct. Centro De Vida, New
  View, Mazzitti & Sullivan, Migliore and White Deer Run of Harrisburg
  are UNVERIFIED from own sites, not closures; third-party "closed" /
  "temporarily closed" flags were not adopted.
- No bed or placement availability is reported. Intake phone/process
  only; availability changes constantly. (One own-site page mentions a
  bed capacity figure for Conewago Place; it is deliberately not
  repeated here as a current availability fact.)
- No treatment or medical advice is given or rewritten. Services are
  described only as the organisations themselves describe them
  (outpatient, residential, halfway house, recovery support, etc.).
- No schedule from an office page is presented as a service schedule,
  and no directory hours are published where they conflict with, or are
  absent from, the organisation's own site.
- No domestic-violence safe-house address appears in this report or
  any file in this folder; none was encountered in this category.
- This report does not refer to Keith as a local resident of Dauphin
  County.
- Town/postal names do not settle county: Lemoyne, Camp Hill and
  Mechanicsburg are Cumberland County and Elizabethtown is Lancaster
  County, even when grouped under "Harrisburg area"; exact address
  decides. Hummelstown (Conewago Place) and Elizabethville are Dauphin
  County.
