# Dauphin County, PA — Child Care / Family Support Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged only, NOT SENT. Nothing in this
audit has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 30 child care / family support listings for
Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is a
curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be
a current list of open services, and it should not be used as one
without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first. No
information about individual children is recorded here. Keith is not
described as a local resident of Dauphin County.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.**
  A lapsed domain, a failed fetch, or a conflicting secondary
  address is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- **Rate-limit limit:** full page fetches succeeded for Capital
  Area Head Start, Studio Kids, Aa to Zz, and Tiny Learners; a
  second batch of fetches then hit a browser-service rate limit
  (429) and was not retried. Remaining own-site evidence rests on
  search-indexed own pages, and verdicts are graded accordingly
  (CONFIRMED only on exact own-site matches). Recheck items
  10–12, 17, 21–24, and 28–30 with full fetches, and re-confirm
  the current Dauphin ELRC intake phone, before any outward send.
- Hours are the weakest field: several organisations' own pages
  state no hours at all while directories confidently give them;
  directory-only hours are labelled as such in the full report.
- Waitlist status is claimed nowhere in this audit — no own site
  retrieved stated one for these listings on 2026-10-25.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections,
  challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211 or the
  organisations themselves are welcome — that is the point of
  staging it first.
