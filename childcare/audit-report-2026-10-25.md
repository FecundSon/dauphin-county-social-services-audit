# Dauphin County, PA — Child Care / Family Support Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-10-25
County: Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area). Category: child care /
family support.
Method: pilot list built from public directories (statewide PA 211
context, secondary lists, PA Pre-K / licence records); every item then
checked against the organisation's own website/page, via public web
search and page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats
a third-party aggregator.
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to statewide
PA 211 or to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before
any outward report. This audit does not refer to Keith as a local
resident of Dauphin County, and it records no information about
individual children.
Counts note: multi-site organisations are counted as in the pilot
list — KinderCare sites separately (own address/phone each),
Hansel & Gretel once for two sites, Aa to Zz once for its Harrisburg
presence. Out-of-county items at the end are NOT in the main count.

## Method limit — read before using the verdicts
Full page-text fetches succeeded for Capital Area Head Start, Studio
Kids, Aa to Zz, and Tiny Learners (domain-for-sale page) on 2026-10-25.
A second batch of page fetches then hit a browser-service rate limit
(429, upstream unavailable); those four fetches were NOT retried, per
the rate-limit rule. Remaining own-site evidence therefore rests on
search-indexed own pages / own-system pages (KinderCare's own finder,
KHS, Hansel & Gretel's own description, the county's own pages, the
Strawberry Square tenant list, and licence records), plus directory
and aggregator claims. Verdicts are graded accordingly: an item is
CONFIRMED only where own-site text matches the directory claim
exactly; where only part of a claim has own-site support it is
CLARIFIED; where no own-site text was retrieved it is UNVERIFIED,
even if several directories agree. A recheck with full fetches is
recommended before any outward send, especially for items 10–12,
17, and 21–24, 28–30.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.

1. **Capital Area Head Start — use the KHS enrollment number, not
   the centre directory number, for intake.** KHS's own Head Start
   page (khs.org, fetched 2026-10-25) says: "Applications for the
   2026-2027 school year are available! … Call 717-541-1795 to learn
   more and enroll." The directory's 1709 N 4th St, Harrisburg,
   (717) 233-7712, Mon–Fri 8:30am–3:00pm is a centre record, not
   the published enrollment route. Ages confirmed on the same own
   page: Early Head Start birth–age 3 (home-based, Harrisburg and
   surrounding Dauphin areas); Head Start ages 3–5, school year,
   5–5.75 hours/day. Dauphin Head Start sites on KHS's own
   resources page include Paxtonia (Lower Paxton), Chambers Hill
   and South Side Elementary (Harrisburg), Banks Street (Penbrook),
   Granite St and HACC (Harrisburg), and Salvation Army
   (Harrisburg); Camp Hill and Carlisle sites are Cumberland
   County and must not be listed as Dauphin. Family Days for
   Early Head Start are at 1654 Walnut St, Harrisburg, PA 17103
   (the New Cumberland Family Days site is Cumberland County).
2. **ELRC for Dauphin is NOT Lancaster's CAP ELRC — do not send
   Dauphin families there.** Dauphin is in ELRC Regions 9 & 10,
   served by Community Connections for Children (CCC, cccforpa.org),
   which lists Dauphin among Adams, Centre, Cumberland, Dauphin,
   Juniata, Lancaster, Lebanon, Mifflin, Northumberland, Perry,
   Snyder, Union, and York counties. An older ELRC map gives
   Region 9 (Cumberland/Dauphin/Lebanon/Perry) as 701 Bosler Ave,
   Suite C, Lemoyne, PA 17043, (717) 210-3334, toll-free
   1-800-864-4925 — note Lemoyne itself is Cumberland County, so
   the office address must not be presented as a Dauphin site.
   Lancaster CAP's ELRC page (601 S Queen St, Lancaster,
   717-393-4004 / 717-509-6797, 717-299-7301) serves a different
   region and must not be substituted for Dauphin. The current
   Dauphin intake phone needs a fresh full-fetch confirmation
   before publication; the toll-free 1-800-864-4925 is the safest
   route to publish in the meantime, flagged as toll-free/regional.
   Local family-centre route: Dauphin County's own Family Centers
   page says to contact the Community Action Commission at
   (717) 232-9757 (Tri County Community Action, 1514 Derry St,
   Harrisburg) — a phone/home-visit route, not a published
   walk-in family centre.
3. **Best Friends Strawberry Square — address corrected.**
   Directory 11 N 3rd St is not the centre's published address.
   Strawberry Square's own tenant/education list gives: Best
   Friends Daycare Center, phone 717-909-7900, address 303 Walnut
   Street, Suite 111, Harrisburg, PA 17101. The PA licence record
   agrees (303 Walnut St Suite 111, capacity 126, subsidized
   programme participates, ELRC Region 9). Phone (717) 909-7900
   and hours Mon–Fri 6:30am–6:00pm are unchanged; ages at this
   site are 6 weeks–5 years (provider description). Publish
   303 Walnut St Suite 111, not 11 N 3rd St.
4. **KinderCare Dauphin sites — ages corrected to 6 weeks–12 years,
   and a fourth Dauphin site added.** KinderCare's own finder
   (kindercare.com, search-indexed 2026-10-25) gives for every
   Dauphin site: ages 6 weeks to 12 years, open 6:30am–6:00pm —
   not the directory description's "6 weeks through 6 years"
   (that narrower band omits before/after-school care). Confirmed
   sites: Hershey, 667 Cherry Dr, Hershey, PA 17033,
   (717) 534-1839; Susquehanna Township, 3701 Vartan Way,
   Harrisburg, PA 17110, (717) 652-3421; Londonderry Road,
   4075 Londonderry Rd, Harrisburg, PA 17109, (717) 657-2495;
   and Jonestown Road, 6006 Jonestown Rd, Harrisburg, PA 17112,
   (717) 652-4348 — the Jonestown site was missing from the
   first directory pass and must be added. Camp Hill
   (3715 Kohler Pl) and Enola (730 Wertzville Rd) KinderCare
   sites on the same finder pages are Cumberland County and
   must not be counted as Dauphin.
5. **Studio Kids — hours are directory-only; do not publish the
   Wednesday early close as own-site fact.** Own homepage
   (studiokidslearning.com, fetched 2026-10-25) confirms:
   1223 N. Cameron Street, 2nd Floor, Harrisburg, PA 17103,
   717-547-6001, ages 6 weeks through school age (infant, toddler,
   preschool, Pre-K, before/after-school, summer camp ages 6–12),
   Keystone STARS Four-Star, and Child Care Works accepted via
   ELRC. It states NO hours. The directory's Mon–Fri
   6:30am–6:00pm, with Wednesday to 3:00pm, is directory-only and
   the Wednesday 3pm close in particular must not be published
   without own-site support. Note for follow-up, not counted
   separately: a PA Pre-K provider list also shows a Studio Kids
   site at 4620 Linglestown Rd, Harrisburg, PA 17112 — do not
   merge it into the Cameron St site without checking it.
6. **Aa to Zz — the Boas St site is directory-only; the
   organisation's own homepage does not list it.** Own homepage
   (aatozz.com, fetched 2026-10-25) says Aa to Zz operates eight
   centres across Dauphin, Lebanon, and York counties, with three
   Harrisburg centres in Hanover Township, Swatara Township,
   and off Route 39, plus Hummelstown at 205 S. John Street,
   Hummelstown, PA 17036, (717) 566-1488. It does NOT list
   651 Boas St, Harrisburg, or (717) 265-7740 in the fetched
   text. Publish the Boas St address/phone only as directory
   claims, and do not present the Hummelstown site as a
   Harrisburg site.
7. **Tiny Learners — own domain is for sale; both sites
   UNVERIFIED, neither is a closure finding.** Fetching
   tinylearners.org on 2026-10-25 returned a GoDaddy
   domain-for-sale page ($2,349), not a centre website. That is
   evidence the old domain has lapsed, NOT evidence either
   centre closed. Locust Ln (2328 Locust Ln, (717) 216-5994,
   6:30am–6:30pm) and N 3rd St (200 N 3rd St #5b,
   (717) 909-2069) are both UNVERIFIED. The aggregator claim
   that the N 3rd St site is "Temporarily Closed" must not be
   republished — it has no own-site support.

## What this report does NOT claim
- No organisation is called closed or defunct. Tiny Learners
  (both sites), New Legends, and every other UNVERIFIED item are
  evidence gaps (lapsed domain, unfetched own page, or conflicting
  secondary addresses), not closure findings, and should be
  rechecked before any outward report.
- Hours, ages, and subsidy routes that appear only in directories
  or aggregators are labelled directory-only in the table and must
  not be published as own-site fact. This is common in this
  category: several organisations' own pages state no hours at
  all (Studio Kids), or give different hours per site (Hansel &
  Gretel), while directories confidently give one schedule.
- Waitlist status is reported nowhere in this audit: no own site
  retrieved stated a waitlist position or "currently enrolling"
  status for these Dauphin listings on 2026-10-25. Do not infer one.
- School-year vs summer hours: where a programme's own page did
  not distinguish them (CAHS school-year programmes, YMCA SACC),
  no summer claim is made. YMCA SACC hours are school-day hours.
- This report gives no child care, health, or benefits advice, and
  records no information about individual children.
- No safe-house or confidential-location address appears in this
  report. None was encountered in this category.
- ELRC subsidy eligibility (income limits, work/training hours)
  is NOT restated here from the Lancaster CAP page, because that
  page serves a different region; only the Dauphin operator/route
  correction is claimed.
