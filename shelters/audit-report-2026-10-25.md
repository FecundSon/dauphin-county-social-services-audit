# Dauphin County Shelter Audit — DRAFT FOR KEITH'S ACCEPTANCE ONLY
**Date checked: 2026-10-25. Not sent to statewide PA 211 or any shelter. Not pushed to any repo.**
**Method:** public web search + page-text fetch only. No shelter, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted. A shelter's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party directories; a directory beats an aggregator. A failed or missing page is reported as UNVERIFIED, never as closure. No shelter in this report is called defunct. No bed availability is reported as a static fact anywhere; intake phone/process only. This report does not refer to Keith as a local resident of Dauphin County.
**Fetch limitation (read this first):** direct page fetches hit a browser-service rate limit part-way through this audit (429 on two fetches). Own-site evidence below therefore comes in two forms, labelled in each row: (a) direct page-text fetch on 2026-10-25, and (b) the organisation's own site as returned by web-search crawl text. Where only (b) was available, the row says so. Nothing in this report rests on a guessed URL: URLs are copied from search results or from a fetched page.

## Headline findings (the ones worth sending on, once Keith accepts)
1. **YWCA Emergency Shelter intake is HELP-only — do not walk in to the admin address.** YWCA's own housing page says: to apply for a YWCA residential programme, call the HELP office at 717-238-2851 and request a housing assessment; after hours, Crisis Intervention 717-232-7511. The 1101 Market St address on YWCA's own site is the organisation's address/phone (717-234-7931) — it is not presented on that page as a shelter walk-in. Directories that list 1101 Market St as the shelter itself should be corrected to HELP intake.
2. **HELP Ministries is the intake hub, not a shelter — and it is the route for two shelters.** Christian Churches United's own HELP page: "HELP is the first point of contact for intake into the following Congregate Emergency Shelters: the YWCA of Greater Harrisburg Emergency Shelter, and Interfaith Family Shelter. Emergency shelter is available on a first-come first-serve basis and is subject to availability." (That last clause is about availability at intake, not a bed count to republish.) HELP: 717-238-2851, CCU HQ 413 S 19th St, Harrisburg. Listing HELP as if it were itself a bedded shelter misdirects people.
3. **Susquehanna Harbor Safe Haven has two distinct offers at one address — do not collapse them.** Own CCU page (fetched 2026-10-25): long-term housing for up to 25 formerly homeless men in Dauphin County, chronic homelessness (typically a year or more) + mental-health diagnosis, contact SHSH@ccuhbg.org; AND a Men's Winter Shelter, open nightly Dec. 1 through March 31, guest arrival 7:30–9:30pm, lights out 10pm, awake 6am, closes 7am, at 1805 N. 12th Street, Harrisburg, PA 17103, phone (717) 232-5029. The winter shelter is seasonal — never present it as year-round, and never present the long-term programme as walk-in emergency shelter.
4. **Downtown Daily Bread's men's night shelter is year-round, with a 7pm line-up — confirmed from its own site text.** Own-site text (search crawl): Men's Emergency Night Shelter (Year-round), line up starts at 7:00pm, front entrance on South Street; Day Shelter & Case Management 7 days/week; lunch daily 12:00–1:00pm; breakfast Mon–Fri 7:00–8:00am. Address 234 South St, Harrisburg, phone 717-238-4717. Directories that describe DDB only as a soup kitchen understate it; directories that give only daytime hours miss the night shelter.
5. **Brethren Housing Association — address and population confirmed from its own site.** Own site (fetched 2026-10-25): 219 Hummel Street, Harrisburg, PA 17104, phone 717-233-6016, email bha@bha-pa.org; "helping families experiencing homelessness since 1989", single parents and children, transitional housing + support (trauma-informed care, financial education, life skills). This is transitional family housing, not a walk-in emergency shelter — do not list it unqualified as emergency shelter.
6. **Shalom House — own domain does NOT currently confirm anything; do not republish its directory entry as verified.** A direct fetch of shalomhouse.net on 2026-10-25 returned unrelated gambling/slot content, not a shelter site. That is not a finding that Shalom House closed — it is a finding that its listed domain cannot currently be used as own-site evidence. Directory claim (9 S 15th St, Harrisburg, 717-232-3482, women + children emergency shelter; SHARP women-veterans transitional housing) is therefore UNVERIFIED from its own site in this audit, and the domain problem itself is worth flagging privately to PA 211 / the organisation before anyone relies on that link. Do not publish the gambling content as the organisation's site.
7. **Salvation Army Harrisburg — no overnight shelter programme found on its own service list.** The Salvation Army's own Harrisburg service listing (search crawl) lists disaster services, emergency financial assistance, food & nutrition, seasonal services, senior clubs, Christian education, women's/youth ministries, worship, and community recreation — no overnight shelter. A third-party assistance page claims shelter/transitional housing "through the HELP office" (717-238-2851). Until the Army or HELP confirms a shelter programme, directories should not list 506 S 29th St, Harrisburg (717-233-6755) as a shelter address; it is confirmed only as the corps/service centre.
8. **Veterans Multi-Service Center, Harrisburg — not a shelter, and its directory phone is a Philadelphia number.** Directory listings pair a Harrisburg address (21 S 3rd St) with phone 215-923-2600 — a Philadelphia-area code, consistent with the Center's Philadelphia base. No shelter programme for Harrisburg was found on its own site in this audit. Correct classification: veterans services/referral, not a Dauphin emergency shelter; Harrisburg office phone UNVERIFIED.
9. **Two "shelters" are transitional/re-entry housing, not general emergency shelter.** Thrive Next Level (young adults 18–24, semi-supervised housing + life skills, own site) and Amiracle4Sure (re-entry mentoring, housing assistance, transitional living support; 1735 State St secondary) serve specific populations/routes. Listing either unqualified as a general emergency shelter would misdirect someone seeking an emergency bed tonight.
10. **Confidential-location flag (count: 1).** The YWCA domestic-violence shelter is counted as a confidential-location flag. Correct public entry: YWCA Greater Harrisburg 24/7 Hotline 1-800-654-1211 (on YWCA's own site footer), admin 717-234-7931, ywcahbg.org. **No shelter street address for it appears in this report.** Any directory publishing a street address for it is a confidential-location listing and must not be copied; report it privately to the directory with the address redacted, never republished as a correction.
11. **Capital Region county trap — "Harrisburg Capital Region" does not mean Dauphin County.** Family Promise of Harrisburg Capital Region's site addresses are in Lemoyne / Camp Hill — Cumberland County — as are Safe Harbour and Community CARES in Carlisle. They serve the region, but they are OUT OF SCOPE for a strict Dauphin-only list. Do not import Cumberland addresses into a Dauphin list, and do not use a "Harrisburg area" label as county evidence.

## What this report does NOT claim
- No shelter is reported as closed/defunct. Every "unverified" item is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding of closure — including Shalom House, whose domain problem is a link/evidence problem, not a closure finding.
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating.
- Bed availability, waitlist position, and "beds available / full" are never stated; capacity figures in secondary sources (e.g. 25/night for the CCU overnight shelter, 25 for Safe Haven long-term) are programme size as stated by the source, not current availability.
- Hours from an office/administrative page are not shelter intake hours, and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (HELP rental-assistance walk-in hours; YWCA admin hours).
- For the YWCA domestic-violence shelter, and for any DV-related listing, no address is given. A directory address for a confidential location is a finding to report privately to the directory, with the address redacted in any public copy — it is not a correction to republish.
- Seasonal dates are only as stated on the organisation's own page on the check date; the Safe Haven winter window (Dec 1–Mar 31) must be re-confirmed for the current season before any send, and Thrive's warming-centre season is UNVERIFIED.
