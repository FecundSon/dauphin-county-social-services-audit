# Dauphin County, PA — Shelter Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged here for Keith's acceptance only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any shelter as a direct message, and nothing has been pushed to any repo.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 22 shelter / transitional-housing listings for Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the organisations' own websites and other public listings, plus 4 regional items recorded as out of county. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open shelters, and it should not be used as one without checking with the shelter or PA 211 first. In particular, it does **not** report bed availability — intake phone and process only, because beds change day to day.

The winter shelter is seasonal only.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No shelter, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A shelter's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party directories. Where a shelter's own site did not state an address, phone, or intake route, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **Fetch limitation:** direct page fetches hit a browser-service rate limit part-way through this audit. Some own-site evidence therefore comes from the organisation's own site as returned by web-search crawl text, labelled as such in the full report. Before any send to PA 211, re-check the Bethesda women/children address, the Salvation Army shelter question, the CCU overnight-shelter season/intake, and Shalom House's current official domain.
- **No shelter is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating.
- Seasonal shelters and intake rules change by year; the Safe Haven winter dates above are as stated on CCU's own current page, and Thrive's warming-centre season is unverified — do not present either as year-round.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211 or the shelters themselves are welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
