# Dauphin County, PA — Housing / Rental Assistance Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged only, NOT SENT. Nothing in this
audit has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation as a direct
message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 26 housing / rental assistance listings serving
Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the organisations'
own websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a
replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
services, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
organisation or PA 211 first. Cross-listed shelter items are flagged as
such; shelter-only entries are not re-audited here.

Counts: 26 items — 5 confirmed, 2 corrected, 15 clarified, 3 unverified,
1 out of scope.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **HELP Ministries RAP — funding is not guaranteed; call first.**
   413 S. 19th St, Harrisburg, PA 17104, 717-238-2851. Back rent /
   move-in help for Dauphin County residents, by appointment (walk-ins
   Mon/Wed/Thu 10:30am and 2:30pm) — but its own site says "there are
   times where RAP funding is not available."
2. **Dauphin County Housing Authority HCV waitlist is CLOSED on
   2026-10-25 (own site).** 501 Mohn St, Steelton, PA 17113,
   717-939-9301. Voucher usable in Dauphin County outside the City of
   Harrisburg only — a separate jurisdiction from Harrisburg Housing
   Authority (City of Harrisburg, 351 Chestnut St, 717-232-6781).
3. **Bridge of Hope Harrisburg Area is NOT emergency rental
   assistance.** 12–24 month programme for single mothers/children;
   referral line **(717) 303-9930**, not the directory phone
   717-635-5957. Intake takes several weeks; no guarantee of
   acceptance.
4. **Dauphin County ERAP is stale — remove it from live help.**
   Paused to new applications March 4, 2022; its later reopening ran
   only July 15–30, 2024. No open programme found on 2026-10-25.
5. **Catholic Charities Harrisburg — do not list as a rent-grant
   provider on this evidence** (4800 Union Deposit Rd, 717-657-4804):
   referrals to housing/shelter and counselling; a secondary summary
   states it does not provide financial aid.
6. **Salvation Army Harrisburg — list by phone, not by a single
   address.** 717-233-6755 is consistent; 506 S. 29th St (service
   centre) vs 1122 Green St (county services page only) is unresolved.
7. **YWCA housing intake runs through HELP, not the YWCA front desk.**
   Call HELP at 717-238-2851 for a housing assessment; YWCA housing
   contact 717-234-7931 ext. 3052, 1101 Market St, Harrisburg.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No service is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
  Exception: waitlist/funding closures stated on an organisation's
  own site on the check date (notably Dauphin County HA HCV) are
  reported as date-stamped own-site statements.
- **Fetch limit:** three own-site page fetches were rate-limited
  (429) and not retried (Shalom House, Brethren Housing transitions,
  CACH permanent housing). Those items are capped at CLARIFIED, never
  CONFIRMED, in the full report — re-check them before sending any
  correction that depends on them alone.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves
  are welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
