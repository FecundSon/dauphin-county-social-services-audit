# Dauphin County, PA — Housing / Rental Assistance Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-10-25
Scope: housing and rental assistance only, serving Dauphin County (Harrisburg
area). This is the candidate list from public directories; findings are in
audit-report-2026-10-25.md (same folder). Nothing has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
report. This task does NOT duplicate a shelters audit's shelter-only
entries; cross-listed items are flagged as such. Keith is NOT described
as a local resident of Dauphin County.

- Capital Area Coalition on Homelessness (cachpa.org / cachpa.com)
- Organisations' own websites / own-domain search results (per item)

## Rules applied to this list
- No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- No waitlist, funding, or seasonal-window status is presented as
  permanent; any such status is date-stamped to the check date
  (2026-10-25).
- No housing, legal, or financial advice is rewritten; contact and
  listing accuracy only.
- No claim that an organisation is closed/defunct is made from a failed
  page load; such items are UNVERIFIED.
- Duplicate programmes (same fund under two names) are flagged, not
  double-counted.
- ZIP code does not settle county — exact address does. Camp Hill is
  in Cumberland County, even where a programme serves Dauphin residents.
- Fetch limitation (see audit report): three own-site page-text fetches
  were rate-limited (429) and were not retried. Items affected by that
  limit are capped at CLARIFIED, never CONFIRMED, in the report.
