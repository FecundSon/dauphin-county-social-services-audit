# Dauphin County, PA — Legal Aid Audit Report
Date checked: 2026-10-25
County: Dauphin County, PA (the Harrisburg area). Category: legal aid
(free civil legal help, legal aid societies, clinics, self-help
resources).
Method: pilot list built from public directories (statewide PA 211,
secondary directories, organisation listings); every item then checked
against the organisation's own website/page, via public web search and
page-text fetches. Own site beats directory; directory beats a
third-party aggregator. The Dauphin County Bar Association's
legal-resources page (dcba-pa.org) is a strong secondary source for
many items but is NOT the organisations' own sites, so items verified
only through it are CLARIFIED, not CONFIRMED, unless the organisation's
own site agrees.
Status: NOT SENT YET. Nothing in this report has been sent to PA 211 or
to any organisation. Keith's accept checkpoint comes before any outward
report. This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident of
Dauphin County.

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site or strong evidence, material to
someone trying to use the service. All other items are in the
per-listing table below.

1. **MidPenn Legal Services, Harrisburg — address corrected, old
   numbers superseded.** Own site (midpenn.org) and the PLAN provider
   directory give **100 North Cameron Street, Suite 401 West,
   Harrisburg, PA 17101**; help line **(800) 326-9177**; administration
   **(717) 234-0492**. The widely repeated **213-A N. Front St.**
   address (Justia and other aggregators) is stale — do not use it.
   Likewise do not use **(717) 232-0581** (still shown on Dauphin
   County's own court FAQ for custody/divorce help) or the old PUC
   pilot number **(800) 932-0356** as current MidPenn numbers. MidPenn
   serves 18 central-PA counties including Dauphin. Phone-intake hours
   are UNVERIFIED on MidPenn's own site — do not publish the
   aggregator's Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm as intake hours; online
   application is available via MidPenn's own site.
2. **YWCA Greater Harrisburg Legal Center — aggregator addresses
   corrected.** PA 211 and the YWCA's own site place the organisation
   at **1101 Market Street, Harrisburg, PA 17103, main (717) 234-7931**,
   with a **24-hour hotline 1-800-654-1211**. The Justia claim
   **112 Market St., 4th Floor** and the aggregator claim
   **114 Walnut St. #2** for the Legal Center are NOT on the YWCA's own
   site — do not publish either as the Legal Center's address. The
   Legal Center's direct number **(717) 724-0516** is also unverified
   on the YWCA's own site; the verified public route is the main number
   and the hotline. The Legal Center gives free civil legal
   representation to victims of domestic violence or sexual assault
   who cannot afford an attorney (PFA, custody, divorce, support,
   powers of attorney, immigration, and more, per PA 211 and the
   YWCA's own programmes page).
3. **Community Justice Project, Harrisburg — own-site phone conflict.**
   CJP's own site lists TWO different Harrisburg numbers: **(717)
   236-9486 / 800-322-7572** (the same numbers as PLAN, with which it
   shares 118 Locust St.) in one listing, and **(717) 710-3834** in
   another. Do not publish either as the single verified Harrisburg
   number until CJP confirms which is current. Address confirmed:
   **118 Locust Street, Harrisburg, PA 17101**. CJP handles only cases
   and advocacy with broad impact on low-income people (including
   class actions) — it is not a general walk-in legal-aid office.
4. **Dauphin County Law Library / Self-Help Center — access conflict.**
   The county's own FAQ places the Self-Help Center in the Law Library
   on the 4th floor of the Dauphin County Courthouse (101 Market St.,
   Harrisburg) and says the Law Library is "only accessible to
   attorneys during regular business hours" and that staff may not
   help fill out self-help packets or give legal advice. That
   conflicts with aggregator claims that the Law Library is "open to
   the public." Do not publish the public-access claim until the
   county confirms it. Self-help forms (custody, divorce, guardianship,
   PFA modification, name change, and others) are also available free
   online via the county site, per the Bar's resources page.
5. **Dauphin County Area Agency on Aging legal help — address
   corrected.** Current county listing and a Widener secondary page
   give **2 South 2nd Street, 3rd Floor, Harrisburg, PA 17101**;
   phone **(717) 780-6130** (or 800-328-0058 ext. 6130). An older
   (2007) directory's **25 South 2nd Street, 3rd Floor** is stale — do
   not use it. Free civil legal help for people over 60 (abuse, age
   discrimination, guardianship defence, health care, housing,
   long-term care, Medicaid/Medicare, Social Security/SSI, utilities).
6. **PFA filing help in Dauphin County runs through Victim/Witness,
   not the courts alone.** Per the Bar's resources page, a walk-in
   Protection from Abuse Clinic is open to the public **8am–4pm** at
   the Victim/Witness main office, **25 South Front Street, 7th Floor,
   Harrisburg, PA 17101, (717) 780-7075, toll-free 888-292-9611**;
   VWAP's own site confirms the address, phone, nonprofit status, and
   free services. The after-hours emergency on-call number
   **(717) 780-8052** appears only in secondary sources — flag it as
   secondary, not own-site-confirmed. VWAP provides advocacy, support,
   information, and referrals — not legal representation.
7. **PA Utility Law Project — do NOT repeat the aggregator's
   "temporarily closed" label.** One aggregator labels PULP
   "Temporarily Closed" and warns against trusting other sources; that
   is an unverified aggregator label, not a finding, and this audit
   does not repeat it as closure. Verified route: utility hotline
   **1-844-645-2500** (Bar's resources page and PULP flyer) and
   utilityhotline@pautilitylawproject.org; the older **(717) 232-2719**
   and the 118 Locust St. address are stale/secondary. PULP's own site
   (pautilitylawproject.org) remains reachable. Current intake status
   should be confirmed with PULP before publishing.
8. **Statewide and out-of-county services must not be presented as
   Dauphin offices.** PLAN (118 Locust St., Harrisburg,
   (717) 236-9486) does NOT provide direct representation — it refers
   to the county programme or PALawHelp.org. PALawHelp.org is a
   referral/info site, not a direct provider. PHLP is statewide only
   (helpline 1-800-274-3258, Mon & Wed 8am–8pm intake; offices only in
   Philadelphia and Pittsburgh). PILP serves people who are
   incarcerated or institutionalised (570-661-9045; offices
   Philadelphia, Lewisburg, Pittsburgh — none in Dauphin) and does not
   assist with criminal matters, habeas, sentencing, or parole. PIRC
   is in York (York County, (717) 600-8099) and serves Dauphin among
   surrounding counties. The Dickinson Community Law Clinic is in
   Carlisle (Cumberland County, (717) 243-2968) — a service point for
   Dauphin residents, not a Dauphin office.
9. **Paid, referral-only, mediation, and information-only services must
   not be presented as free legal aid.** DCBA Lawyer Referral
   (213 N. Front St., Harrisburg, (717) 232-7536) is a paid referral
   to private attorneys; its $51.50 consultation-fee figure appears
   only in a secondary source and is unverified on the Bar's own
   site. DCBA Modest Means is reduced-fee, not free (2024 form:
   $500 retainer upfront, $50/hour, filing fees extra; trial-level
   cases only, listed case types only). DCBA Pro Bono is a referral
   service, not a walk-in office. Neighborhood Dispute Settlement
   (717-233-8255) is free mediation for custody and landlord/tenant
   issues, not representation. PA Safe Law (833-727-2335) gives
   information, resources, and referrals and does NOT represent
   clients. SeniorLAW (877-727-7529, Mon–Thu 10am–12pm) is a
   telephone service; extended individual representation is, per a
   secondary source, limited to the Philadelphia-area counties.
10. **PA Free Legal Answers — eligibility-threshold conflict.** The
    programme is confirmed operating in Pennsylvania
    (pa.freelegalanswers.org: qualifying low-income users post civil
    questions for volunteer attorneys; brief advice only, no
    long-term representation). But sources conflict on the income
    threshold: the programme's own site gave under 250% of federal
    poverty level (per the 2026-10-24 Lehigh audit), while a Widener
    secondary page says below 200%. Do not publish either figure as
    settled for Dauphin until the programme's own site is rechecked.

## Out-of-county / straddle flags
- Carlisle trap: Penn State Dickinson's Community Law Clinic is in
  Carlisle, Cumberland County — do not label it a Dauphin office.
  The Carlisle Barracks JAG office is likewise Cumberland County and
  military-only.
- York trap: PIRC is in York, York County — do not label it a Dauphin
  office, even though its rapid-response coverage includes Dauphin.
- Harrisburg trap: Harrisburg is the Dauphin County seat, but several
  Harrisburg addresses in this list are statewide headquarters, not
  Dauphin County offices: PLAN, CJP's shared PLAN address, PHRC
  Harrisburg Regional Office, and the Fairness Center's statewide
  practice. Statewide-only (no Dauphin office): PHLP (Philadelphia and
  Pittsburgh), PILP (Philadelphia, Lewisburg, Pittsburgh), SeniorLAW
  extended representation (Philadelphia-area counties, secondary).
- ZIP does not settle county for any listing near the Dauphin/
  Cumberland or Dauphin/York lines.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. A failed
  page load, a dead link, or an unconfirmed listing is reported as
  UNVERIFIED, never as closure. In particular: the aggregator's
  "temporarily closed" label for PULP is not adopted, and PULP's own
  site remains reachable.
- It does not publish intake hours as office hours, or office hours as
  intake hours (MidPenn is the clearest case: no phone-intake hours
  were verified on its own site).
- It does not publish stale figures as current: MidPenn's 213-A N.
  Front St. address and (717) 232-0581 number, the AAA's 25 S. 2nd St.
  address, PULP's (717) 232-2719 number, the Bar's secondary $51.50
  consultation fee, and Free Legal Answers' conflicting income
  thresholds are all flagged as dated or conflicting, not republished
  as 2026 facts.
- It does not present paid referral, court/administrative services,
  victim support, mediation, research/advocacy, or referral-only and
  information-only services as free legal aid. Items 5, 6, 7, 11, 12,
  13, 14, 25, and 28 are qualified in the table for exactly that reason.
- It does not give legal advice, and it does not summarise or rewrite
  any organisation's legal advice. It audits contact information only.
- It does not give a shelter/safe-house address for the YWCA, and it
  does not give a Dauphin County location to any Carlisle or York
  listing.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating at the listed address or hours.
