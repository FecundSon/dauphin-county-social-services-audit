# Dauphin County, PA — Legal Aid Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged here only, NOT SENT. Nothing in
this audit has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct
message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 30 legal aid listings for Dauphin County, PA
(the Harrisburg area), checked against the organisations' own websites
and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement
directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open services, and
it should not be used as one without checking with the organisation or
PA 211 first. Keith is not a local resident of Dauphin County, and this
audit does not describe him as one.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)

   Suite 401 West, Harrisburg, PA 17101** — not the widely repeated
   213-A N. Front St. Help line **(800) 326-9177**, admin
   **(717) 234-0492**. Do not use (717) 232-0581 (still on the county
   court FAQ) or (800) 932-0356 (old PUC pilot number). Phone-intake
   hours are unverified on MidPenn's own site.
2. YWCA Greater Harrisburg Legal Center: the organisation's public
   base is **1101 Market Street, Harrisburg, PA 17103, (717) 234-7931,
   24-hour hotline 1-800-654-1211**. Do not publish the aggregator
   addresses 112 Market St. 4th Floor or 114 Walnut St. #2, and do not
   publish the direct (717) 724-0516 as own-site-verified. Free civil
   representation for victims of domestic violence / sexual assault
   who cannot afford an attorney.
3. Community Justice Project, Harrisburg: **118 Locust Street,
   Harrisburg, PA 17101** is confirmed, but its own site lists two
   Harrisburg numbers — (717) 236-9486 / 800-322-7572 and
   (717) 710-3834. Neither should be published as the single current
   number until CJP confirms. Impact/class-action cases only, not a

4. Dauphin County Law Library / Self-Help Center: Self-Help is in the
   Law Library, 4th floor, Dauphin County Courthouse, 101 Market St.,
   Harrisburg; staff give no legal advice and may not help fill out

   during regular hours — this conflicts with aggregator
   "open to the public" claims and is unresolved.
5. Dauphin County Area Agency on Aging legal help: **2 South 2nd
   Street, 3rd Floor, Harrisburg, (717) 780-6130** (or 800-328-0058
   ext. 6130) — not the older 25 South 2nd Street address. Free civil
   help for people over 60.
6. PFA filing help runs through Victim/Witness: walk-in PFA Clinic
   **8am–4pm, 25 South Front Street, 7th Floor, Harrisburg,
   (717) 780-7075, toll-free 888-292-9611**. VWAP gives advocacy and
   support, not legal representation. The after-hours number
   (717) 780-8052 is secondary-source only.
7. PA Utility Law Project: do NOT repeat the aggregator's
   "temporarily closed" label — it is unverified, and PULP's own site
   remains reachable. Verified route: hotline **1-844-645-2500**.
8. Statewide and out-of-county services must not be presented as
   Dauphin offices: PLAN (no direct representation), PALawHelp.org
   (info/referral only), PHLP (statewide, Philadelphia/Pittsburgh
   only), PILP (incarcerated/institutionalised only; no Dauphin
   office), PIRC (York), Dickinson Community Law Clinic (Carlisle,
   Cumberland County).
9. Paid, referral-only, mediation, and information-only services must
   not be presented as free legal aid: DCBA Lawyer Referral (paid),
   DCBA Modest Means (reduced-fee: $500 retainer, $50/hour per its
   2024 form), DCBA Pro Bono (referral only), Neighborhood Dispute
   Settlement (mediation, not representation), PA Safe Law (info and
   referrals, does NOT represent), SeniorLAW (telephone service).
10. PA Free Legal Answers is confirmed operating in Pennsylvania, but
    its income threshold is in conflict: own-site 250% of poverty vs
    a secondary page's 200%. Recheck before publishing either figure.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. The Dauphin County Bar Association's
  legal-resources page is a strong secondary source but is not the
  organisations' own sites, so items verified only through it are
  marked CLARIFIED, not CONFIRMED.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page, or an aggregator's "closed" label, is
  reported as unverified, never as closure.
- No legal advice is rewritten here; contact information only.
- No shelter or safe-house address appears in any file.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome
  — that is the point of staging it here first.
