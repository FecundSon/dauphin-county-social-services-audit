## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send
These are the items with own-site evidence, material to someone trying
to use the service. All other items are in the per-listing table below.
Fares and schedules are dated 2026-10-25 and must not be republished as
permanent facts.

1. **Dauphin County MATP has a new administrator: rabbittransit, not
   CCB, since 2026-07-01 — and existing CCB enrollees must reapply.**
   Dauphin County's own transportation page still says "The Center for
   Community Building enrolls and coordinates Medical Assistance trips.
   717-232-7009." rabbittransit's own May 2026 advisory-committee
   minutes record: "the center for community building will be closing
   its doors and rabbittransit will be the Medical Assistance
   Transportation Program (MATP) Administrator for Dauphin County,
   effective July 1, 2026"; customers could begin calling rabbittransit
   on June 15 to schedule trips on or after July 1; June mileage
   submissions went to rabbittransit by an early-July deadline; and
   "Consumers currently enrolled in Dauphin County MATP through CCB
   will need to reapply through rabbittransit." Any listing that still
   sends a new MATP applicant to CCB (717-232-7009) is stale. The
   current route is rabbittransit, 1-800-632-9063, via its shared-ride /
   Find My Ride application. (Items 4–5.)
2. **"Capital Area Transit (CAT)" is a retired brand — use
   rabbittransit, 1-800-632-9063, not 717-233-5657.**
   CAT was integrated into the Susquehanna Regional Transportation
   Authority with rabbittransit in November 2021; CAT branding continues
   on buses with a sticker "a service of rabbittransit". The old
   directory number 717-233-5657 (still in the Prevent Blindness low-
   vision directory as "Cumberland-Dauphin-Harrisburg Capital Area
   Transit") should not be published as current. Current: rabbittransit,
   901 N Cameron St, Harrisburg, PA 17101, 1-800-632-9063. (Item 20.)
3. **Capital Region fixed-route fare (dated 2026-10-25): $2.00 regular,
   $1.80 rabbitPAY pre-paid, $1.00 half fare; seniors free with a
   registered ID; children under 44" free with an adult.**
   rabbittransit's own fares page gives those figures and states cash
   fares are exact change only, with no change cards issued — a change
   from older CAT-era practice. The county page's "free to persons age
   65 and older Monday through Saturday" should not be published
   without the registered-ID condition: rabbittransit requires a Free
   Fare Registered ID card (proof of age at a rabbittransit office or
   Strawberry Square). Do not publish a bare "$1.80" as the cash fare —
   $1.80 is the rabbitPAY pre-paid price; cash is $2.00. (Item 1.)
4. **Greyhound and Trailways share one Harrisburg address — 411 Market
   St — and it is not a separate Greyhound terminal.**
   411 Market St is the Harrisburg Transportation Center's bus level
   (4th & Chestnut complex). Greyhound's own station page gives
   411 Market St, 1-800-231-2222; a second Greyhound page gives
   717-255-6970 — verify a single local number before publishing one.
   Trailways gives the same 411 Market St address with
   1-800-858-8555, plus a curbside stop at 326 Chestnut St and an
   airport stop at 1 Terminal Dr, Middletown. Do not list Greyhound,
   Trailways, and "Harrisburg Bus Terminal" as three different stations.
   (Items 16–18.)
5. **Medical transport is not general public transport — three NEMT
   items need relabelling.**
   Boyo Transportation (Lower Swatara, Dauphin County) is NEMT and a
   former MATP contractor, not a book-it-yourself public bus; MATP trips
   now go through rabbittransit (finding 1), not direct to Boyo.
   Integrated Medical Transport is based at 1600 Hummel Ave, Camp Hill
   — Cumberland County, not Dauphin — and its Dauphin coverage is not
   own-site confirmed. Commonwealth Transport is Lancaster-based
   (554 W King St, Lancaster) serving Dauphin among Central PA counties;
   it is a private NEMT firm (24/7, price-on-application), not a county
   programme. List all three as NEMT with their real base, never as
   Dauphin County public transport. (Items 21–23.)
6. **Do not use the medhomeinfo.org "Dauphin County Medical Assistance
   Transportation" page as a listing.**
   It claims 24/7 door-to-door MAT service and "call at least 48 hours
   in advance", but its phone is printed as 1-800-XXXXXXX — a
   placeholder, not a number. It is a third-party marketing page, not an
   operator, and must not be copied into any directory. (Item 27.)

## What this report does NOT claim
- No organisation or service in this report is called closed or defunct.
  Items marked UNVERIFIED (VOA Dial-A-Driver; the three Harrisburg taxi
  firms) are gaps in public own-site evidence, not findings, and must
  not be published as fact or as closures.
- Fares and schedules are dated 2026-10-25. rabbittransit's $2.00 /
  $1.80 / $1.00 fare structure, the volunteer programmes' service
  windows, and all intercity times must be re-checked before
  republication; routes, fares, and contract operators change — the
  MATP administrator change of 2026-07-01 is the live example.
- No vehicle or ride availability is reported as a static fact.
- No eligibility or benefits advice is rewritten here; MATP, shared-
  ride, and senior-ride eligibility are reported only as the
  organisations themselves state them.
- No domestic-violence safe-house address appears in this report.
  No confidential location was encountered in this category.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating (see items 5 and 24, where undated or thin own-site pages
  are the only evidence).
- Keith is not a local resident of Dauphin County; nothing in this
  report claims otherwise.
