# Dauphin County, PA — Transportation Audit Pilot
Date started: 2026-10-25. Check date for all findings: 2026-10-25.
County: Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area). Category: transportation —
public transit, paratransit/shared ride, senior/disability rides, medical
transport, volunteer rides, intercity bus/rail, airport ground transport.
Scope: services serving Dauphin County residents/riders. A Harrisburg,
Hershey, Middletown, or Lower Swatara address does not by itself settle
county — exact address/township was checked where it mattered (see items
22–23: Camp Hill is Cumberland County; Lancaster is Lancaster County).
This file is the candidate list from public directories; findings are in
audit-report-2026-10-25.md (same folder). Nothing has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation. No organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
