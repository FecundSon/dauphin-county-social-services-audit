# Dauphin County, PA — Transportation Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged only. Nothing in this audit has
been sent to PA 211 or any organisation.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 27 transportation listings serving Dauphin
County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the organisations' own
websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a
replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
services, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
organisation or PA 211 first. Fares and schedules are date-stamped to
2026-10-25 and must not be presented as permanent. Keith is not
described as a local resident of Dauphin County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **Dauphin County MATP administrator changed 2026-07-01: rabbittransit,
   not the Center for Community Building.** Dauphin County's page still
   sends MATP applicants to CCB, 717-232-7009. rabbittransit's own May
   2026 minutes say CCB is closing and rabbittransit is the MATP
   administrator from July 1, 2026, and that existing CCB enrollees must
   reapply through rabbittransit (1-800-632-9063, Find My Ride).
2. **"Capital Area Transit (CAT)" is a retired brand.** CAT joined
   SRTA/rabbittransit in November 2021. Do not publish the old directory
   number 717-233-5657; use rabbittransit, 1-800-632-9063, 901 N Cameron
   St, Harrisburg.
3. **Capital Region fixed-route fare (2026-10-25): $2.00 cash, $1.80
   rabbitPAY pre-paid, $1.00 half fare; seniors free only with a Free
   Fare Registered ID card; children under 44" free with an adult.**
   Cash is exact change only, no change cards. Do not publish "$1.80"
   as the cash fare.
4. **Greyhound and Trailways share one Harrisburg address — 411 Market
   St, the Transportation Center bus level — not separate terminals.**
   Greyhound 1-800-231-2222 (second number 717-255-6970 conflicts —
   verify); Trailways 1-800-858-8555, plus curbside 326 Chestnut St and
   airport stop 1 Terminal Dr, Middletown. Do not merge in old
   Fullington numbers (1-800-942-8287 / 814-765-7871).
5. **Medical transport ≠ general public transport.** Boyo (Lower
   Swatara, Dauphin County) is NEMT, booked via MATP/rabbittransit, not
   a public bus. Integrated Medical Transport is Camp Hill
   (Cumberland County) based; Commonwealth Transport is Lancaster
   based — both serve Dauphin only as NEMT, labelled with their real base.
6. **Do not copy the medhomeinfo.org "Dauphin County MATP" page into any
   directory.** Its phone is printed as 1-800-XXXXXXX — a placeholder
   on a marketing page, not an operator.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No service is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- Fetch limitation: direct page fetches hit an upstream rate limit
  (429) part-way through checking. Own-site evidence for several items
  therefore rests on search-index snippets from the organisation's own
  domain, and aggregator-only items are marked UNVERIFIED. Re-fetch the
  rabbittransit MATP/shared-ride, Greyhound, and Trailways pages and
  resolve the conflicts in the audit report before any PA 211 submission.
- Fares and schedules change; re-check rabbittransit fares, office
  hours, Route 7 airport times, and the MATP route before republishing.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of publishing it here first.
