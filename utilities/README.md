# Dauphin County, PA — Utilities Assistance Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged here first for statewide PA 211
and the organisations to respond to. Nothing in this audit has been sent
to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message. No repo push has been
made.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 28 utilities-assistance listings for Dauphin
County, PA (Harrisburg area), checked against the organisations' own
websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a
replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
programmes, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
organisation or PA 211 first. Seasonal, funding, and waitlist statuses
are date-stamped to 2026-10-25 and must not be presented as permanent.
This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident of Dauphin County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. LIHEAP is NOT yet open on 2026-10-25 — the 2026–27 season opens
   November 2, 2026, and closes April 23, 2027. 2025–26 ran Dec 3, 2025–
   May 8, 2026 and is closed. Route: COMPASS, hotline 1-866-857-7095
   Mon–Fri. Preseason COMPASS applications are invitation-only, not
   general intake.
2. Dauphin County Assistance Office: 2432 N 7th St, Harrisburg, PA 17110,
   717-787-2324, Mon–Fri 8am–5pm. The separate "LIHEAP line"
   717-265-8919 is directory-only — publish the main number and the
   statewide hotline instead.
3. PPL phone split: PPL's own CARES / OnTrack / Operation HELP routes
   give 1-800-342-5775. 1-800-358-6623 is a real PPL number but appears
   as the payment-arrangements line in PUC listings, not as PPL's
   current own-site CARES number.
4. PPL Operation HELP income limit is conflicted (200% in older sources,
   250% in newer STEP/PUC material) — ask PPL/PA 211 to confirm rather
   than publishing one figure as settled.
5. Salvation Army Harrisburg: use 506 S 29th St, Harrisburg, PA 17104
   (not the older 1122 Green St directory address) and 717-233-6755
   ext 135 for utility referrals — and do not republish its utility
   page's 2025 income tables as current (UGI CAP 150% is $23,940 for
   one person in 2026–27, not $23,475).
6. Dollar Energy Fund 2026–27 IS open (Oct 1, 2026–Sep 30, 2027,
   first-come, first-served, MyApp Connect, 1-888-282-6816) — do not
   confuse it with LIHEAP, which is not yet open. FirstEnergy's Dollar
   Energy hardship windows differ by season and service status.
7. Met-Ed / FirstEnergy in Dauphin County is fringe only — PCAP, WARM
   and hardship programmes are real, but no own-site map ties Met-Ed
   to specific Dauphin municipalities. Publish only with
   "check your bill for your distributor."
8. Water is three doors, not one: Capital Region Water (Harrisburg city,
   888-510-0606, programme detail directory-level), Pennsylvania
   American Water H2O Help to Others (via Dollar Energy, up to
   $500/year, ≤250% FPIG, PA American customers only), and Veolia Water
   PA CAP via SHARES (below 200% FPL, Dauphin/Perry townships).
9. LIHWAP is stale — last window July–August 2023; do not list as open.
10. Catholic Charities Harrisburg is a referral route, not a direct
    utility payer, on the evidence found (4800 Union Deposit Rd,
    717-657-4804).
11. PA PUC line 1-800-692-7380 is a regulator complaint route, not
    bill-payment assistance; payment arrangements / budget billing are
    all-customer options, distinct from income-based CAP/OnTrack/PCAP.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No programme is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- No benefits, eligibility, or financial advice is rewritten here;
  eligibility is recorded only as the organisation states it.
- Dauphin County is not one utility territory: check the bill for the
  distributor (PPL / UGI / Met-Ed / Capital Region Water / PA American
  Water / Veolia). ZIP code does not settle county.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of staging it here first.
