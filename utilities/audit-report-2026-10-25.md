(LIHEAP, shut-off help, fuel funds, weatherization, water/sewer assistance).**

**Method:** public web search + page-text fetch only. No organisation, PA 211,

   November 2, 2026, and closes April 23, 2027 (date-stamped).**
   DHS's own apply page still carries the 2025–26 text ("From December 3,
   2025, to May 8, 2026, you can apply") alongside the banner "The
   2026-2027 LIHEAP Season is scheduled to open on November 2, 2026" and
   "The 2025-2026 LIHEAP SEASON IS NOW CLOSED." The FY 2027 state plan
   confirms: cash and crisis both open November 2, 2026, close April 23,
   2027, income ≤150% FPIG, cash $200–$1,000, crisis $25–$1,000. Any
   listing presenting LIHEAP as currently open on 2026-10-25, or
   presenting the 2025–26 season as current, is stale. Preseason COMPASS
   applications (from August 24, 2026, for prior-season recipients with a
   mailed registration number) are invitation-only and are NOT general
   intake. Route confirmed: COMPASS, hotline 1-866-857-7095 Mon–Fri,
   Relay 711. Note a conflicting date in a FirstEnergy bill insert
   (November 1, 2026–April 9, 2027): use the DHS dates above, not the
   utility insert's dates.
2. **Dauphin County Assistance Office — address and main phone confirmed;

   (c) Veolia Water Pennsylvania CAP (via SHARES): monthly discount for
   income below 200% FPL, conservation kits, possible plumbing repairs

   Veolia serves Dauphin/Perry areas including Marysville, Susquehanna,

9. **LIHWAP — stale, do not list as open.** As in Lehigh County: last

11. **Two miscategorisations to fix.** (a) The PA PUC line 1-800-692-7380
