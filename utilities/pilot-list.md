# Dauphin County Utilities Assistance — Pilot List
County: Dauphin County, PA (Harrisburg area). Category: utilities assistance
(LIHEAP, shut-off help, fuel funds, weatherization, water/sewer assistance).
Source directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org),
supplemented from utility and administrator programme pages.
Date compiled: 2026-10-25.
Status: DRAFT FOR KEITH'S ACCEPTANCE ONLY. Nothing sent to PA 211 or any
organisation. Own-site checks and verdicts are in
audit-report-2026-10-25.md. This list does not refer to Keith as a local
resident of Dauphin County.

Note on counting: utilities assistance is programme-based, not
premises-based, so several listings are the same programme reached through
different doors (e.g. LIHEAP via DHS, via the County Assistance Office, and
via a utility; CAP via the utility and via a community administrator).
Those are listed separately because directories list them separately, and
the audit flags the duplicates rather than hiding them.

Note on territory: Dauphin County is not one utility territory. Harrisburg
city water/sewer is Capital Region Water; much of the county's electricity
is PPL; gas is largely UGI; Veolia Water and Pennsylvania American Water
serve parts of the county outside the city; Met-Ed (FirstEnergy) is at most
a fringe provider here — check the bill for the distributor, never assume
county-wide coverage. ZIP code does not settle county — exact-address
geocoding does.
