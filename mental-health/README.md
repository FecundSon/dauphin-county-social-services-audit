# Dauphin County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-10-25. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent
to PA 211 or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 27 mental health / crisis listings for Dauphin County, PA
(the Harrisburg area), checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open services, and it should
not be used as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211
first. Contact information only — no mental-health advice, diagnosis,
or treatment guidance is rewritten here.

Counts: 27 items — 10 confirmed, 3 corrected, 8 clarified, 5
unverified, 1 out of scope.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-10-25. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories.
- **No service is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- One county own-page fetch (Dauphin County "How To Access Mental Health
  Services") hit a browser-service rate limit on 2026-10-25 and was not
  retried; item 5 rests on that page's search extract plus the PA.gov
  county list and needs a live-browser re-check before any PA 211 submission.
- Other re-checks before any PA 211 submission (live browser): Merakey
  Harrisburg own location page, PPI moved locations, JFS and Pressley Ridge
  own contact pages, PMHCA and Holcomb own sites.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of staging it here first.
- This audit does not refer to Keith as a local resident. Directory for
  any future send: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org) — NOT
  PA 211 East, NOT the Lancaster database manager.
