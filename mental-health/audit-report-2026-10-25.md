## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. UNVERIFIED
  means "not confirmed from the organisation's own site on 2026-10-25",
  including where a site was live but published no phone, address, or hours.
- It does not claim directory hours as organisation hours where the
  organisation's own site states none.
- It does not claim the PMHCA or Holcomb phones/addresses above are wrong
  in every digit — it claims they are unconfirmed and conflicting, and
  must be re-checked against the organisations' own sites before use.
- It does not publish bed, placement, or waitlist availability, and it
  does not rewrite any mental-health advice.
- It does not give a street address for any domestic-violence safe house,
  and it does not claim any listing's county from ZIP or city names —
  Camp Hill / Mechanicsburg / Wormleysburg exclusions were checked by
  municipality/county, and Hershey, Paxtang, Hummelstown, Lower Paxton
  and Susquehanna Township inclusions are Dauphin County municipalities.
- It does not refer to Keith as a local resident.
- It does not replace 988 with the county line or the county line with 988.
